## Step 4: Clarify Unresolved Requirements

Ask only about purpose, audience, scope, constraints, or expected behavior that the request and workspace have not resolved. Reuse earlier answers and choose reversible details yourself.

Use `AskUserQuestion` or the current runtime's structured user-input tool when exposed and permitted. Follow its schema; do not invent an "Other" field or option when the tool supplies its own. If a structured tool is unavailable or prohibited for this question, ask one concise question in prose.

Bundle related decisions when they can be answered independently. Ask sequentially when one answer determines the next question. With an asynchronous question, continue independent work while required input remains pending; do not turn elapsed time into an answer.

Give concrete alternatives and practical trade-offs when those clarify the choice. Use open prose for a genuinely open product question rather than forcing a multiple-choice form.

Stop when the intended product and important constraints are clear enough to draft. Further questions should resolve a new material ambiguity, not complete an intake quota.
