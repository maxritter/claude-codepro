## Step 5: Propose Approaches

Compare plausible approaches when they involve consequential differences in scope, user experience, data ownership, or cost. Explain the useful trade-off and recommend one.

Do not manufacture alternatives when the user already chose a direction or the workspace establishes a straightforward solution. Choose reversible implementation details yourself and record consequential assumptions.

Ask for a selection only when the remaining choice materially changes the requested product and cannot be inferred safely. Use the current runtime's permitted question mechanism. Combine related approach and scope decisions rather than asking twice; otherwise proceed to Step 6 and draft the PRD.
